Version 1.4.1

New:
• Optional gesture trail — a soft stroke follows your finger while you swipe and fades out when you lift it. Off by default, under Settings › Gestures
• New app icon: Liquid Glass on iOS 26, with depth on the Home Screen

Improved:
• Fast flicks are recognized as swipes again instead of typing the key's center letter
• Characters that could not be typed at all on the new layouts: Thai ฬ ฒ ฑ ฏ and ฿, the Japanese 、 and 。 with the small kana, the half-space in Persian and Urdu, Arabic-script punctuation, the Hindi ₹, the Hebrew ׳ and ״, and the voiced kana iteration marks ゞ and ヾ
• č ď ě ň ř š ť ž are directly reachable: the caron now sits on the return swipe of the ^ key
• Diagonal drags on the delete key delete instead of doing nothing
• A long press held across a mode or language switch no longer types the old key's digit
• Double-space types a period only on two consecutive spaces, and inserts the sentence mark of the script you are typing
• Forward-delete and capitalize-word respect an active selection
• Coming back from another app applies settings changed in the meantime before the first keystroke, and the app switcher no longer shows an empty gap where the keyboard was
• VoiceOver reaches the shift, punctuation and mode keys, and offers swipe-only actions such as copy, cut, paste and hide keyboard through the rotor
• Key labels stay inside their key at the smallest keyboard sizes
• Size & Position was shown in English whatever the app language, and is translated now, along with 70 corrected translations
• Leaner rendering leaves the keyboard more memory headroom
